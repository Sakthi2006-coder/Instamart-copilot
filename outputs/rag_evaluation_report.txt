3. THEORETICAL FOUNDATION: TF-IDF & LIMITATIONS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Term Frequency (TF): Measures the local prominence of a term within an individual chunk, normalizing for chunk length so longer passages are not unfairly favored.
  2. Inverse Document Frequency (IDF): Measures the global rarity and discriminatory power of a term across the entire chunk corpus, downweighting ubiquitous filler words and upweighting terms unique to specific policies.
  3. Key Limitation for Policy Retrieval: TF-IDF relies exclusively on surface lexical overlap. It fails to match synonymous queries where users express identical semantic intent using distinct vocabulary (e.g., 'reimbursement' vs 'refund', or 'courier' vs 'rider'), leading to zero similarity scores when exact word forms do not align.
