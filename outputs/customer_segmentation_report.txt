3. FINAL K SELECTION & BUSINESS JUSTIFICATION

Why K=8 inertia is lower than K=2 yet cannot alone pick K:
  Mathematically, inertia (the within-cluster sum of squared errors) is monotonically decreasing with respect to K because adding more cluster centroids always reduces or preserves the Euclidean distance to the nearest centroid, trivially reaching zero when K equals sample size N.
