Model Selection & Trade-Off Justification:
  Random Forest is selected as the final production model. In grocery quick-commerce, missing an at-risk customer (a false negative) results in permanent churn and lost customer lifetime value, whereas acquiring a replacement customer is significantly more costly than sending retention vouchers. Conversely, incorrectly targeting a loyal customer (a false positive) incurs only minimal coupon costs without harming the relationship. Random Forest delivers superior 5-fold cross-validation performance (0.6865 vs 0.6506 for Logistic Regression) by capturing non-linear interactions across recency, basket size, and delivery duration, while maintaining balanced test precision and recall (F1 = 0.6404 >= 0.55 requirement).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Top 5 Most Important Features in Tuned Random Forest:
  1. monetary               (Importance: 0.2778 / 27.8%): Cumulative spend before cutoff is the primary driver of retention commitment; larger historical spend anchors brand loyalty.
  2. frequency              (Importance: 0.2768 / 27.7%): Order count indicates habitual platform usage; higher purchase cadence significantly suppresses churn risk.
  3. recency                (Importance: 0.1112 / 11.1%): Days since last completed order directly signals purchase dormancy; inactivity is an immediate warning of impending lapse.
  4. avg_delivery_minutes   (Importance: 0.0616 / 6.2%): Average fulfillment duration reflects operational service quality; chronic delivery delays trigger customer defection.
  5. avg_discount_pct       (Importance: 0.0532 / 5.3%): Promotional dependency reflects price sensitivity; discount-seeking users lapse when incentives taper off.

Key Logistic Regression Coefficients (Feature Signs & Business Readings):
  - frequency    (Coefficient: -1.6982): Sign is NEGATIVE (-1.6982). Higher purchase frequency substantially decreases the log-odds of lapsing; frequent shoppers are habitual, sticky users.

  - monetary     (Coefficient: +0.2846): Sign is POSITIVE (+0.2846). Holding frequency and recency constant, higher basket amounts correlate with occasional, high-ticket electronics purchases rather than weekly essentials, slightly increasing lapse probability.
